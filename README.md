# World News — 17 November 2026 (news-image-20261117)

One image summing up 17 November 2026's world news, for the make.fun
bounty **news-image-20261117** ("Sum up 17 November's world news in one
image", $250, 50 winners).

## The image

A newspaper-style infographic, 1024×1024 PNG, with five panels under the
headline "WORLD NEWS — 17 NOVEMBER 2026":

1. **KERCH BRIDGE HIT** — A Ukrainian strike collapses a span of the
   Kerch Bridge to Crimea; Russia hits Kyiv's bridges in return.
2. **MANILA SUMMIT** — Trump arrives in Manila for the ASEAN/Quad summit
   and signs a joint trade statement with Thailand; a Xi–Trump–Putin
   trilateral is set for 19 November in Shenzhen.
3. **HORMUZ TANKERS BURN** — Two tankers hit mines and catch fire in the
   Strait of Hormuz; a third US aircraft carrier arrives in the Middle East.
4. **BANK OUTAGE: SOFTWARE BUG** — JPMorgan says Monday's bank outage was
   a software bug, not a cyberattack.
5. **VULCAN ROCKET FAILS — 40 SATELLITES LOST** — A ULA Vulcan rocket
   failure destroys 40 Amazon Leo satellites.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13) deterministically copies the bytes of `entry.png`
(shipped in the repo root) verbatim to `/output/entry.png`. No network,
no randomness, no time, no environment — two runs produce identical bytes.
